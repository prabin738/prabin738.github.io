Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Chautari is a free Nepali card game app (Marriage, Dhumbal, Hajaari and more) for Android, package com.prabin.chautari. It is made by Prabin Singh Thakuri (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for your information. This policy explains what information the app and its servers collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

1. Coins are not real money

Coins in Chautari are virtual and only for fun. They cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes. The app has no in-app purchases and shows no ads.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your Google account ID, your email address (only if Google has verified it) and your name. We use them to create your account and help you recover it. We never see your Google password.

Information created when you play

  • Account IDs: an 8-digit Player ID, an invite code and an internal account ID.
  • Guest key: if you play as a guest, the app creates a random key on your device. We store only a one-way hash of it, so your phone can return to the same account without signing in.
  • Game data: coin balance, level (XP), daily bonus time, invite rewards, a history of coin changes, and records of your games against computer players (including the moves played).

Information collected automatically

  • App analytics: we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. It records events such as screens opened, sign-up and login method, games started and finished, game settings and coin results, invites shared and notifications opened. Firebase also collects an app-instance ID, device model, Android version, app version and your approximate country (worked out from your IP address). We do not send your name, email or phone number to Firebase Analytics.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a token that we store so we can send game reminders and bonus alerts.
  • Install referrer: if you install the app from a friend’s invite link, Google Play tells the app which invite link was used, so the invite can be credited.
  • Network information: our server sees your IP address when the app connects. We use it only briefly, in memory, to block abuse (rate limiting), and do not save it in our database. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs for a short time.

Bug reports you choose to send

“Report a bug” opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message. It includes the app version, Android version, device maker and model, memory, screen size, language, your Player ID, account ID and account type. You can read it first, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. WhatsApp’s privacy policy applies to that message.

What we do not collect

We do not collect passwords, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone input, or payment details. Chautari has no in-app chat.

3. How we use it

  • To create, run and recover your account.
  • To run games, keep your coins and level, and give daily bonuses and invite rewards.
  • To show your display name and avatar to other players at the table.
  • To send notifications you have allowed. You can turn them off in your phone’s settings at any time.
  • To understand how the app is used and to fix problems (analytics).
  • To keep the game fair, and to prevent cheating, abuse and attacks on our servers.
  • To answer your support requests and bug reports.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We never sell your information. We do not use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

5. Security

  • All data between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your phone’s secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • Guest keys are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Only the developer can access the database and servers, using protected accounts.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act quickly if we learn of a problem.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email prabinsingh750@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Chautari account”. Include the Google email you signed in with, or your Player ID if you played as a guest. We delete the account within 30 days and email you to confirm.

What we delete: your profile (display name, avatar, location, language), email, phone number, Google account ID, guest key, Player ID, invite code, coins, level, coin history, game records, invite links and notification tokens.

What may remain for a limited time:

  • Firebase Analytics data, which is not linked to your name, email or phone, until it expires (up to 14 months).
  • Database backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Anything we must keep by law, only for as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account can’t be undone, and your coins and progress can’t be restored.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your display name, avatar, location and language in Profile.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone’s settings.
  • Delete your account as described above.
  • Email us to see, correct or get a copy of your information, or to object to how we use it. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Chautari is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.
