Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Chautari is a free Nepali card game app (Marriage, Dhumbal, Hajaari and more) made by Prabin Singh Thakuri. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Coins are not real money

Coins in Chautari are virtual and only for fun. They cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes.

What we collect

  • Guest play: a random key created on your device, which we store only in hashed form, so you can come back to the same account without signing in.
  • Google sign-in (optional): your Google account ID, your Google-verified email address and your name. The email is kept only to help recover your account.
  • Profile details you choose to add: display name, avatar, location, phone number and language.
  • Game data: your coin balance, level (XP), daily bonus time and a history of coin changes.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. The app shows no ads and has no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To run your account, keep your coins and level, and let you play.
  • To show your display name and avatar to other players at the table.
  • To keep the game fair and prevent abuse.

We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who processes it

Your data is stored on our servers and database hosting providers, who keep it on our behalf. Google sign-in is provided by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Device keys are stored only as hashes.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, coins, level and coin history from our servers. If you can't open the app, email us from your Google account's email address and we will delete it for you.

Children

Chautari is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
