Delete your Chautari account

Chautari · developed by Prabin Singh Thakuri

You can delete your Chautari account and the data linked to it at any time. You don’t need the app installed to ask us.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Chautari and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you’ve uninstalled the app or can’t open it, email us with the subject “Delete my Chautari account”.

Email prabinsingh750@gmail.com

In your email, include:

  • If you signed in with Google: send the email from that Google address, or tell us what it is.
  • If you played as a guest: your 8-digit Player ID (shown on the Profile screen). If you don’t have it, tell us your display name and roughly when you last played.

We may ask one question to check the account is yours. We will never ask for a password. We delete the account within 30 days and email you to confirm.

What we delete

  • Your profile: display name, avatar, location and language
  • Your sign-in and contact details: email, phone number, Google account ID and guest key
  • Your Player ID and invite code
  • Your coins, level, coin history and game records
  • Your invite links and notification tokens

What we keep, and for how long

  • App analytics in Google Firebase, which is not linked to your name, email or phone, is deleted automatically within 14 months.
  • Database backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Your deletion request email is kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Anything we must keep by law is kept only as long as the law requires.

Before you delete

Deleting your account can’t be undone. Your coins and progress can’t be restored. You can always start again with a new account.
